Overview

Camposer

Kotlin Multiplatform Camera Library

Camposer is a Compose Multiplatform camera library that gives you a modern camera stack for Android and iOS: photo capture, video recording, flash/torch controls, zoom, focus, stabilization, and analyzers.

Platform Support

Camposer is a Kotlin Multiplatform library supporting:

Platform Status
Android ✅ Supported
iOS ✅ Supported
